Suasage Egg and Cheese

Mmmmm what's that smell? Its savory and strong, bold even but in a welcoming way. It is hugging my nose and stomach. I can smell the eggs and suasage. Who is making such a magnificant meal?

Ingredients

  • Beef Suasage
  • 3 Eggs
  • Cheddar Cheese
  • Italian roll
  • Mayo
  • Ketchup
  • Salt
  • Black Pepper

Steps

  1. Crack 3 Eggs into a bowl and wisk until mixed
  2. Add a pinch of salt and pepper to your egg
  3. Fry your beef suasage patties for 10 minute
  4. Cut your Italian Roll in half to stage your sandwich
  5. Butter you pan after you suasage is finished and pour egg yolk into pan
  6. Fry eggs for about 3 minutes
  7. (optional)Spread Mayo and ketchup on roll
  8. assemble sandwich
